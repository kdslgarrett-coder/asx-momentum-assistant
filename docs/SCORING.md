# MomentumHQ Analyst Scoring Model

## Purpose

This document defines how the MomentumHQ Analyst evaluates opportunities.

It does not define implementation details or algorithms.

Instead it defines the reasoning process used to determine whether an opportunity deserves an investor's attention.

---

# The Analyst's Objective

The MomentumHQ Analyst answers one question.

> **What deserves my attention today, and why?**

Every recommendation must be supported by evidence.

Confidence is earned by multiple pieces of evidence agreeing with each other.

---

# Analyst Workflow

Every opportunity is evaluated in the following order.

## 1. Detect a Catalyst

A catalyst is an event capable of changing investor behaviour.

Examples include:

- Resource upgrade
- Major contract
- Earnings surprise
- Trading halt lifted
- Acquisition
- Capital raising
- Regulatory approval
- Commodity price movement
- Sector momentum

Without a catalyst there is usually no reason for significant price movement.

---

## 2. Determine Catalyst Quality

Not all catalysts are equally important.

Examples:

High Impact

- Resource upgrade
- Major contract
- Earnings beat
- Significant discovery
- Regulatory approval

Medium Impact

- Quarterly report
- Investor presentation
- Operational update

Low Impact

- Director interest notice
- Appendix filings
- Administrative announcements

The Analyst should recognise the difference.

---

## 3. Seek Market Confirmation

This is the most important stage.

MomentumHQ should never recommend a stock simply because an announcement exists.

The market must confirm that the catalyst matters.

Evidence may include:

- Price appreciation
- Relative volume expansion
- Breakout above resistance
- Strong buying pressure
- Sustained momentum

Without confirmation, confidence should remain low regardless of the announcement.

---

## 4. Evaluate Technical Health

Technical analysis supports the decision.

It should never dominate the decision.

Examples include:

- Trend
- EMA alignment
- VWAP
- Relative strength
- Momentum
- Breakout quality

Technical indicators improve confidence.

They do not create opportunities.

---

## 5. Evaluate Risk

Every opportunity carries risk.

Examples include:

- Low liquidity
- Large spreads
- Excessive volatility
- Capital raising risk
- Weak market conditions

Higher risk reduces confidence.

---

## 6. Produce Confidence

Confidence represents the Analyst's belief that an opportunity deserves further investigation.

Confidence should be based on:

- Quality of the catalyst
- Strength of market confirmation
- Technical health
- Risk assessment

Confidence should never rely on a single signal.

---

# Confidence Ratings

95–100

Exceptional opportunity.

Rare.

Strong catalyst.

Strong confirmation.

High conviction.

---

80–94

High confidence.

Deserves immediate investigation.

---

65–79

Good opportunity.

Worth watching closely.

---

50–64

Interesting.

Requires additional confirmation.

---

Below 50

Insufficient evidence.

Do not recommend.

---

# Analyst Narrative

Every recommendation should explain:

## What happened?

Describe the catalyst.

---

## Why does it matter?

Explain the business significance.

---

## What is the market doing?

Explain price, volume and confirmation.

---

## What are the risks?

Provide a balanced assessment.

---

## Why should the investor care?

Explain why the opportunity deserves attention today.

---

# Design Principles

The Analyst should:

- Prefer quality over quantity.
- Ignore market noise.
- Reward confirmation over speculation.
- Explain every recommendation.
- Reduce investor research time.
- Present only actionable opportunities.

---

# Future Learning

The scoring model should improve over time.

Future versions may learn:

- Which catalyst types produce the strongest momentum.
- Which sectors respond fastest.
- Which confirmations are most reliable.
- Which signals historically lead to successful opportunities.

MomentumHQ should become smarter with experience.

---

# Golden Rule

A catalyst without market confirmation is information.

A catalyst with strong market confirmation is an opportunity.

MomentumHQ exists to recognise the difference.

---

# Engineering Principle

The Analyst should never ask investors to interpret technical indicators.

The Analyst should interpret the evidence and explain the conclusion in plain English.

MomentumHQ should transform complexity into confidence.

---

# Motto

**Detect. Confirm. Explain. Prioritise.**
