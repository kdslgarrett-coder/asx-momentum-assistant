# MomentumHQ

## Opportunity Intelligence for ASX Investors

---

# Vision

MomentumHQ is an Opportunity Intelligence platform for ASX investors.

Our mission is to continuously monitor the Australian share market, identify meaningful catalysts, determine whether the market is confirming those catalysts, and present only the opportunities that deserve an investor's attention.

MomentumHQ is designed to reduce information overload by acting as the investor's own analyst.

Instead of requiring users to interpret charts, indicators and news, MomentumHQ performs the analysis and explains why a stock deserves further investigation.

---

# Mission

Help investors answer one question:

> **What deserves my attention today, and why?**

If MomentumHQ answers that question well every day, it is succeeding.

---

# Product Philosophy

Markets generate enormous amounts of information.

Most of it is noise.

MomentumHQ exists to filter that noise and surface only meaningful opportunities.

Our role is not to predict the future.

Our role is to recognise when the market is validating an opportunity.

---

# Guiding Principles

## 1. Catalyst First

Every opportunity begins with a catalyst.

Examples include:

- Resource upgrades
- Major contracts
- Earnings
- Trading halts
- Acquisitions
- Regulatory approvals
- Commodity price movements
- Sector momentum

Without a catalyst there is rarely a reason for significant momentum.

---

## 2. Market Confirmation

Announcements alone do not create opportunities.

The market must confirm the catalyst.

Confirmation may include:

- Price appreciation
- Increased volume
- Relative volume expansion
- Breakouts
- Sustained buying pressure

The market determines whether a catalyst matters.

---

## 3. Confidence Over Indicators

Users should never need to interpret dozens of technical indicators.

Indicators exist to improve confidence.

They are inputs into the analyst—not outputs presented to the user.

MomentumHQ converts many technical signals into one confidence assessment.

---

## 4. Explain Every Opportunity

Every opportunity must answer four questions.

### What happened?

Describe the catalyst.

### Why does it matter?

Explain the business impact.

### What is the market doing?

Describe price, volume and confirmation.

### Why should I care?

Explain why this deserves attention now.

---

## 5. Decision Support

MomentumHQ does not tell investors what to buy or sell.

Instead it provides evidence-based decision support.

Typical recommendations include:

- Investigate immediately
- Worth watching
- Wait for confirmation
- Ignore

The investment decision always remains with the investor.

---

# What MomentumHQ Is Not

MomentumHQ is not:

- A charting package
- A technical analysis application
- Another stock screener
- A news reader
- An AI chatbot

Charts, indicators and news are supporting evidence—not the product.

---

# What MomentumHQ Is

MomentumHQ is an analyst.

It continuously monitors the ASX, evaluates market behaviour and presents only the opportunities that deserve attention.

---

# The Analyst Workflow

Every trading day MomentumHQ asks:

1. What happened?

2. Does it matter?

3. Is the market confirming it?

4. Is it investable?

5. Why should the investor care?

Only then does it recommend that an investor investigate a stock.

---

# Engineering Principles

Every feature must improve one of these capabilities.

## Detect

Find meaningful catalysts.

## Confirm

Measure the market's reaction.

## Explain

Generate an understandable narrative.

## Prioritise

Rank opportunities by confidence.

## Simplify

Reduce complexity for the investor.

If a feature does not support one of these principles, it should not be added.

---

# Product Goal

MomentumHQ should become the first application an investor opens each morning.

Within minutes the investor should know:

- Which stocks deserve attention.
- Why they deserve attention.
- What the market is doing.
- Which opportunities are worth investigating further.

MomentumHQ should save investors time by performing the first layer of analysis for them.

# MomentumHQ Analyst Principles

The MomentumHQ Analyst should behave like an experienced market analyst.

It should never overwhelm the investor with data.

Instead it should transform thousands of pieces of market information into a small number of high-confidence opportunities.

Every recommendation should answer:

- What happened?
- Why does it matter?
- Is the market confirming the catalyst?
- What are the risks?
- Why does this deserve my attention today?

If the Analyst cannot clearly explain why a stock deserves attention, it should not recommend it.

The quality of opportunities is more important than the quantity of opportunities.

MomentumHQ exists to save investors time, reduce information overload and improve decision making.
